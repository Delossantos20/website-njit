Welcome to My NJIT Webpage

My name is Yoel and I’m a Junior at NJIT studying IT with a concentration in cybersecurity. My interest in cybersecurity derived from how complex networks are and methods to protect them. I started out with interests mainly in IT or computer repair, but as time went by I started taking classes for IT and earning certifications such as Comptias’ A+, Security + and practicing for network + and CYSA+. I also spend most of my time experimenting with new technologies such as testing LM models,building and testing environments, or experimenting with new ideas to fulfill my sanity of wanting to know everything. Environments I've constructed are client to server, server to server, client to client. To understand how traffic moves across a network, I used an attacker device and a victim device to safely simulate attacks in a controlled environment which was all monitored using Splunk from a separate machine, most times on a separate network.

Outside of academics, I spend most of my time gaming, fishing, or taking long drives. Before studying IT, I served in the military where I performed basic diagnostics on Motorola radios.I handled functional testing, troubleshooting common issues, and making sure the radios were mission‑ready. I also worked with ILDs, the interior locking devices used on secure doors, where I replaced components and verified proper operation. Working hands-on with these mechanical systems built my interest in how technology functions, which eventually led me to learning about networks and information systems.
